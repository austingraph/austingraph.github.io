# austingraph — 1-Page Strategy Memo

_June 2026_

---

## The Problem

Austin's HOME ordinance unlocked by-right density on ~90% of residential lots, but acting on
any specific parcel still requires 4-6 hours of manual research: TCAD appraisal pull, zoning
lookups, permit history, FEMA flood check, watershed/impervious caps, envelope math. Developers
and investors are making decisions on gut instinct or skipping marginal deals because the
friction isn't worth it.

## The Product

**austingraph collapses that 4-6 hours into one click.**

Click any of 374,000 Travis County parcels → instant panel: dimensions, zoning, development
envelope (setbacks, buildable area, max units, FAR), Site Check flags (flood, watershed,
jurisdiction), appraisal/tax, neighborhood comps, live permit history, zoning cases, and a
full pro-forma feasibility report with three switchable scenarios (build/hold/residual land
value). Everything a developer or land investor needs to triage a parcel before committing
to a deeper look.

Competitive moat: Texas is a non-disclosure state (no public sale prices), so no Zillow/CoStar
clone works here. Our advantage is PostGIS spatial intelligence + live civic data + HOME
ordinance math that generic tools don't do.

---

## Wedge: Infill Developers + Land Investors

**Who pays first:** 50-200 active Austin infill builders + land brokers who are evaluating
parcels daily. They are time-poor, deal-oriented, and already paying $200-500/mo for inferior
data stacks. Secondary wedge: architects and urban planners doing due diligence on client sites.

**Why they'll pay:** The feasibility report + Site Check flags replace ~$500 of consultant time
per parcel. At $39-59/mo they break even on the first parcel they avoid a mistake on.

## Monetization

| Tier | Price | Unlocks |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Free** | $0 | Map, parcel facts, dimensions, planning context |
| **Pro** | $39–59 /mo | Feasibility reports, Site Check, $/sqft comps, CSV export |
| **Business** | $149–299 /mo | Team seats, bulk lead-list exports, API access |
| **Pay-per-report** | $15–25 /report | One-off for non-subscribers |

Target: 50 Pro subscribers = $2,000-3,000 MRR within 6 months. That validates a $500k+ ARR
ceiling before raising or expanding beyond Austin.

## Go-to-Market Sequence

1. **Founder-led outreach (now):** 10-15 discovery calls with Austin developers, land
   brokers, and architects. Watch them use the live app. Listen for "I'd pay for this today."
   Don't pitch — diagnose. Refine the feature priority and pricing from what they say.

2. **Programmatic SEO (month 2-3):** One indexed page per parcel address
   (`austingraph.chat/parcel/123-main-st-austin`). Austin development searches are high-intent
   and underserved. Target "Austin parcel zoning," "[address] HOME eligibility," etc.

3. **Product-led virality (month 3+):** Shareable feasibility report URLs. Every report a
   Pro user sends to a client or partner is a free marketing impression. Add a "Generated by
   austingraph" footer on free reports.

4. **Partnerships (month 4-6):** Title companies (they touch every transaction), REALTORS
   with investor-client books, lenders underwriting ADU/infill loans. White-label or referral
   arrangements.

## Validation Checkpoints

- **Signal 1:** 3+ people in discovery calls say "send me the link to sign up" unprompted.
- **Signal 2:** 10 paying subscribers within 60 days of launching the paywall.
- **Signal 3:** Organic traffic growing without paid spend by month 4.
- **Kill switch:** If <5 paying users after 90 days of active outreach, reassess ICP before
  expanding features.
