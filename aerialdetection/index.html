Reading these findings

Every flag is advisory, not a determination. Measurements come from imagery of a specific vintage and capture date, which is stamped on each writeup; conditions may have changed. Claude never invents a measurement — numbers are computed in PostGIS and passed to the model as structured input, and the narrative cites them. Treat output as a prioritized list of parcels worth a human look, nothing more.
